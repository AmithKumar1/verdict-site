⚖️ Editorial Independence: Funding Does Not Buy Coverage
INVIOLABLE STANDARD

Verdict operates under strict newsroom independence. A financial contribution funds forensic research hours, statutory lookup costs, and archival retrieval. Funding does not determine Verdict's findings, editorial treatment, publication decisions, or conclusions.

Funded research remains subject to the exact same evidence thresholds, multi-source corroboration, and neutral presentation standards as every other investigation on the platform.

✕ No favorable coverage: Funding cannot purchase praise, soft framing, or positive spin.
✕ No removal of facts: Payment can never buy the deletion or suppression of documented public records.
✕ No predetermined outcomes: The evidence dictates the finding, never the sponsor or requester.
✕ No guaranteed publication: If public records do not support the claim, Verdict will not publish accusations.

Public Transparency Standard

How Funded Investigations Disclose External Support

Whenever an investigation or entity dossier on Verdict is initiated or sustained via a funded research request, the published record carries a permanent, immutable third-party funding disclosure:

THIRD-PARTY FUNDING DISCLOSURE VERIFIED ON-CHAIN · BASE TX: 0x4f8a...c912

Editorial Disclosure: This investigation was requested and funded by a third party. Under Verdict's Editorial Independence Policy, third-party funding does not determine Verdict's findings, editorial treatment, publication decisions, or conclusions. All evidentiary claims remain independently corroborated via primary statutory filings and court proceedings.

Questions & Clarifications

Frequently Asked Questions

What happens if no public evidence is found?

Verdict will document the negative finding and evidentiary absence. If an allegation lacks verifiable public documentation, Verdict will not publish uncorroborated accusations. The research log is maintained in the internal audit trail.

Can someone pay to remove an existing investigation?

No. Under no circumstances can anyone pay to suppress, alter, or delete a published dossier or documented public record. Records are updated solely through verified corrections submitted with primary statutory evidence.

Can I fund an investigation anonymously?

Yes. Cryptographic transactions are pseudonymous by nature. In the submission form, you can choose to be disclosed as "Anonymous Third-Party Contributor" or by your public wallet address.

How long does an investigation take?

Eligibility reviews take 24–48 hours. Depending on the complexity and the responsiveness of public statutory registries and court archives, investigative dossiers typically take between 5 to 14 business days.
