Submit Evidence, Corrections or Leads

Our investigative dossiers are living records. If you have identified primary source filings, corrections to dates, unrecorded relationships, or evidence gaps, submit your documentation below.

Forensic Integrity Notice: All submissions must be backed by verifiable, public records or documented filings. Do not submit credentials, passwords, unverified hearsay, or private communications. Submissions are staged for editorial review before appearing in canonical publications.
✓ What Qualifies:
  • Official gazettes & court rulings
  • Certified MCA / RoC corporate filings
  • Original charters & public filings
  • Archived web pages with immutable URLs
  • Reporting by named journalists
✕ What Does NOT Qualify:
  • Anonymous rumors or unverified tips
  • Stolen passwords or leaked databases
  • Non-consensual private messages/chats
  • Screenshots without provenance/context
  • Partisan accusations without proof
