Research Architecture

How Verdict Works: From Public Question to Auditable Record

Conventional search engines rank pages to produce summaries. VERDICT executes an evidence-graph loop: converting auditable questions into targeted sweeps, normalizing observations, detecting contradictions, and applying a human publication review gate.

STEP 01

1. Discover

Decomposes research target into objective classes: identity, corporate MCA records, gazettes, archives, and public statements.

STEP 02

2. Normalize

Extracts atomic observations with publication date, author byline, retrieval hash, and source classification.

STEP 03

3. Corroborate

Resolves identities across multi-token matches. Flags contradictory reports with opposite negation polarity.

STEP 04

4. Review Gate

Enforces privacy safeguards. Excludes raw private vaults or credentials. Human editorial review verifies evidentiary thresholds.

STEP 05

5. Publish

Renders public projections with explicit epistemic labels: Documented, Reported, or Unresolved Evidence Gap.

Epistemic Restraint

What VERDICT Does NOT Infer

To prevent defamation, bias, and false attribution, our research engine enforces hard negative boundaries. We explicitly refuse to infer intent, hidden coordination, or guilt from circumstantial proximity.

Proximity ≠ Coordination

Two entities attending the same protest or appearing in the same public photo does not establish organizational alignment or conspiracy.

Funding Pledge ≠ Receipt

A senior advocate announcing legal aid does not establish transfer of funds to an individual or proof of personal enrichment.

Past Employment ≠ Present Affiliation

Documented campaign work in 2020 does not establish ongoing institutional control in 2026 without primary ongoing evidence.

"Not Found" ≠ "Does Not Exist"

A missing record in our searched corpus is recorded as a bounded absence, not as positive proof that a fact is impossible.

AUDIT & EDITORIAL STANDARDS

Institutional Standards & Algorithmic Transparency

Automated systems may discover, classify, or flag candidate material. Publication decisions strictly require human evidence review and are never determined solely by automation.

1. First-Class Evidence Packets

Every published claim satisfies the 10-second test: "Why do we believe this?", identifying primary sources, independent corroborations, counter-evidence, and bounded evidence gaps.

2. Cryptographic Preservation

Sources are archived to independent repositories (Wayback Machine / Archive.today) with SHA-256 integrity hashes to defend against link rot and revisionism.

3. Right-of-Reply System

Subjects are contacted through documented channels before publication. Responses are published verbatim with neutral editorial attribution, following Bellingcat standards.

4. Non-Mutating Audit Trail

All revisions, source additions, and challenge requests are immutably logged. Challenges are routed to a verified human review queue rather than directly mutating canonical records.

Aligned with investigative standards from Bellingcat, Global Investigative Journalism Network (GIJN), and ICIJ. Review Full Protocols ↗
